# Anvil-Lang Traction Strategy

## Goal
Reach $180K ARR (50 teams at $300/mo) within 18 months.

## Pillars

### 1. Interactive Playground
- **Objective**: Reduce friction to zero for trying Anvil.
- **Action**: Deploy `frontend/index.html` to a public URL (e.g., Vercel) and connect it to a live Anvil backend.
- **Target**: Web3 developers who want to see formal verification in action without installing Rust/Z3.

### 2. "Why Trust Doesn't Compile" Content Series
- **Objective**: Educate the community on the value of formal verification.
- **Action**: Write deep-dives on famous hacks (The DAO, Wormhole, K2) and show how Anvil invariants prevent them.
- **Platform**: Substack (Vector 3) and X (Twitter).

### 3. Case Studies & Outreach
- **Objective**: Build social proof.
- **Action**: Offer free formal verification audits for small/medium protocols in exchange for case studies and testimonials.
- **Target**: 50 paying teams.

## Metrics to Track
- Playground conversion rate (visitors → proofs run).
- GitHub stars and forks.
- Inbound inquiries for the SaaS.
